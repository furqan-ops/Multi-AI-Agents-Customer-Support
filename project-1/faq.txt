NEXIO24 Support FAQ

Opening Hours:
We are open 24/7 for urgent support. Standard support hours are 9 AM to 6 PM Monday through Friday.

Refund Policy:
Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase. Refunds over  require manager approval.

Booking Policy:
Bookings can be made up to 7 days in advance. Cancellations must be made 24 hours before the booking time.

Contact:
For urgent issues, call our support line. For non-urgent, email support@nexio24.com.

Password Reset:
To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be emailed to you within 5 minutes.
